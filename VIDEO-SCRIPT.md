# The Invisible Payment — demo video script (60 seconds, SIMULATION)

A video is not strictly required for the Tari Build Contest — this
script is for the announcement post (Bluesky, Reddit, Medium or
YouTube) if Eddie films one. Film the live demo page on the phone
(screen recording), in landscape.

**Everything in this video is the SIMULATION.** No real TARI moves.
Say the word "simulation" out loud at least twice, and keep the page's
own SIMULATION label visible on screen the whole time. Never present
the simulation as a live on-chain reveal.

One sentence a viewer should repeat afterwards: **"Money nobody can
see until the receiver types the secret."**

## 0:00–0:05 — Hook (sound off: it must work with no audio)

On screen before you say anything: the demo page showing the vault as
invisible — no amount, no sender, no receiver.

On-screen text (large, bottom third): **MONEY NOBODY CAN SEE.**

Say: "This vault holds money. You cannot see how much, or whose it
is. This is The Invisible Payment — a simulation."

## 0:05–0:15 — Problem, one sentence, one named user

Say: "Ada wants to send money to her brother, but she does not want
the whole world watching the chain to see it. So she hides it until
he is ready."

On-screen text: **SIMULATION — NO REAL TARI MOVES.**

## 0:15–0:45 — The one live flow (30 seconds, simulation)

Narrate what is happening, not the labels on screen.

1. Say: "She locks the payment in the vault, names her brother, and
   sets the reveal time. The secret never touches the chain — only a
   fingerprint of it." Show the vault as invisible on screen.
2. Say: "Try to open it early, and it refuses." Show the reveal
   blocked before the epoch.
3. Say: "Try the wrong secret, and it refuses." Show the wrong-secret
   rejection.
4. Say: "Now the time comes. He types the secret — and the money
   appears." Type the secret, and show the balance appearing on
   screen. That is the loud moment. Say: "Only the receiver, only
   with the secret, only after the time. That is the whole trick."

## 0:45–0:60 — How it works, and what's next (15 seconds)

Say: "Underneath, it is three real checks on Tari Ootle: the time,
the secret, and the receiver's key. The live version is done too.
It locked 1 TARI in the vault and the receiver revealed it on-chain
on 27 November. Try the simulation yourself at the link below."

On-screen text: **THE INVISIBLE PAYMENT — TARI BUILD CONTEST · SIMULATION DEMO**

End on the balance-appearing screen. Stop recording.

## Honest limits — say them if asked, do not hide them

- The simulation is a simulation. No real TARI moves in the browser
  demo. The on-chain template is real Rust code, its 10 engine tests
  pass, and it is published live on Esmeralda (27 Nov 2026).
- The live reveal is DONE on Esmeralda (27 Nov 2026): 1 TARI locked
  in the vault and revealed by the receiver, tx
  06595f82498fc7942314b75e8c36d43d59602d8fa5e3243788019639febf3c93,
  epoch 13449. The faucet stayed dry throughout; the reveal was
  funded by the entrant herself.
- The invisibility is the stealth property of native TARI on Ootle:
  amounts and owners of stealth resources are hidden from observers.

## Recording notes (phone)

- Turn on Do Not Disturb on the phone first, so no notification lands
  mid-take.
- Film in landscape. Browser zoom 100–125%, full screen, bookmarks bar
  hidden, extra tabs closed. Open the live demo page:
  https://skymiller1509-blip.github.io/invisible-payment/
- Use the phone's built-in Android screen recorder: swipe down twice,
  tap Screen record, microphone on, wait for the countdown.
- Keep the page's SIMULATION label in frame for the whole take.
- Add captions — the video may be watched without sound.
- Rehearse out loud at least three times before the take you keep.
  Sixty seconds is short; if a take runs long, cut the "how it works"
  line, never the reveal.
